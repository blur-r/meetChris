Project Overview

Problem Statement

Sending and receiving money across borders is often slow. costly and confusing. Users struggle with high fees. poor exchange rates, and lack of trust in existing platforms

Goal of the Product

Design a seamless mobile app that enables users to send, receive and manage international payments quickly, securely, and transparently.

Design Goals

  • Simple onboarding with identity verification
  • Clear transaction flow
  • Transparent fees and exchange rate upfronts
  • Secure but fast aunthentication
  • Multi-currency wallet for global use
